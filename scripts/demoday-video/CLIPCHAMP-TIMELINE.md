## Zoom method in Clipchamp

Clipchamp does not offer a complete automatic camera path. Reproduce the movement by splitting the clip around the action:

1. Keep the first segment at 100% scale.
2. Set the action segment between 108% and 116%.
3. Reposition the crop so the target remains near the centre.
4. Add a short 0.15–0.25 s cross-fade only when the visual jump is noticeable.
5. Return to 100–103% after the action.

Do not exceed 120% on the 1920 × 1080 recordings unless a very small interface element must be highlighted.

## Text style

- Use one sans-serif typeface throughout.
- Prefer medium or semibold weight.
- Maximum two lines.
- Keep each text visible for 2.5–4 seconds.
- Use deep green on ivory backgrounds and ivory on deep-green backgrounds.
- Reserve orange for a single word, underline or short accent.

## Music

Choose a track available inside Clipchamp with these characteristics:

- modern corporate or subtle electronic style;
- no vocals;
- no dramatic beat drops;
- approximately 90–110 BPM;
- a clean ending or an easy fade-out.

The music must support the product demonstration, not dictate the edit.

## Final review checklist

- No terminal, PowerShell or VS Code is visible.
- No browser address bar is visible in the final crop.
- The cursor remains visible and purposeful.
- Every click has an understandable result.
- No loading screen lasts more than 0.5 s.
- French interface text remains readable.
- English editorial text never hides an important action.
- The supplier-to-store-to-request story is understandable without voice-over.
- Final duration remains below 90 seconds.
